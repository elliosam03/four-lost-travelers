You are in charge of progressing a team of four unique characters through an underground dungeon. 

They will not make it through on their first go.

Luckily for you, the characters will keep their stats and items upon death.

Follow the prompts within. Good luck :) 


Enemies inspired from various video games.
Character stat progression and Merchant inspired by Elden Ring.
Combat sequence and character weight inspired by Forge of Empires.
